# Essential Eight

## Overview

The Essential Eight is a set of mitigation strategies developed by the Australian Cyber Security Centre (ACSC) to help organisations protect themselves against common cyber threats.

It provides a practical baseline for improving cyber resilience and reducing the likelihood and impact of common attacks.

---

## The Eight Mitigation Strategies

### 1. Application Control

Control which applications are permitted to execute within an organisation.

**Objective:** Prevent unauthorised or malicious applications from running.

---

### 2. Patch Applications

Ensure security vulnerabilities in applications are identified and remediated within defined timeframes.

**Objective:** Reduce exposure to known application vulnerabilities.

---

### 3. Configure Microsoft Office Macro Settings

Restrict potentially malicious Microsoft Office macros and only allow trusted macros where business requirements justify their use.

**Objective:** Reduce the risk of malicious code being executed through Office documents.

---

### 4. User Application Hardening

Harden commonly targeted applications such as web browsers and PDF software.

**Objective:** Reduce opportunities for attackers to exploit application features.

---

### 5. Restrict Administrative Privileges

Limit administrative access and ensure privileged accounts are tightly controlled.

**Objective:** Reduce the potential impact of compromised credentials and privilege escalation.

---

### 6. Patch Operating Systems

Ensure operating systems are regularly patched and vulnerabilities are remediated within appropriate timeframes.

**Objective:** Reduce exposure to known operating system vulnerabilities.

---

### 7. Multi-Factor Authentication

Require additional authentication factors for access to important systems and services.

**Objective:** Reduce the likelihood of account compromise when passwords are stolen or guessed.

---

### 8. Regular Backups

Maintain regular, protected and tested backups of important information and systems.

**Objective:** Enable recovery from ransomware, destructive attacks and other incidents.

---

## Implementation Approach

A practical Essential Eight implementation should consider:

- Current maturity
- Business risk
- Critical systems
- Regulatory requirements
- Technical capability
- Resource constraints
- Third-party dependencies
- Existing security controls

Implementation should be risk-based rather than treated as a simple compliance exercise.

---

## Maturity Assessment

Organisations should periodically assess their maturity against the Essential Eight and establish a roadmap for improvement.

A typical approach is:

**Assess → Prioritise → Remediate → Validate → Monitor → Improve**

---

## Executive Perspective

The Essential Eight should be viewed as one component of a broader cybersecurity strategy.

Effective cyber resilience also requires:

- Strong identity management
- Security monitoring
- Incident response
- Third-party risk management
- Security awareness
- Data protection
- Business continuity
- Disaster recovery
- Governance and accountability
